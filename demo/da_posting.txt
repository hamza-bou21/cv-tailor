Role: Data Analyst — Growth & Operations
Company: Northstar Retail
Location: Remote

About the Role:
We are looking for a Data Analyst to own recurring KPI reporting, build self-serve BI dashboards, and partner with product and operations teams on data-driven experiments.

Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain automated reporting pipelines and executive dashboards in Power BI or Looker Studio.
- Write complex SQL queries (BigQuery or Snowflake) to aggregate large transactional datasets and optimize query costs.
- Use Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn) to automate manual reporting workflows and build predictive models for customer and delivery operations.
- Partner with product managers and UX teams to evaluate onboarding flows and post-launch feature performance.
- Monitor data pipeline health and enforce validation checks to prevent reporting discrepancies.

Requirements:
- 1–3 years of experience in data analysis, business analysis, or analytics operations.
- Strong proficiency in SQL, Python, and modern BI tools (Power BI, Looker Studio, Excel).
- Experience modeling tabular data (e.g., Random Forest, XGBoost) and handling class imbalance.
- Familiarity with dbt and Snowflake is a plus.
- Clear written communication in English; working proficiency in French is an advantage.
